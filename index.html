Three fixes. Zero lag.

macOS has one Natural Scrolling switch for every device, and a couple of habits that trip up games. Mouse Gaming Mode fixes them and then gets out of the way.

↕︎

Wheel like a PC, trackpad like a Mac

Pick Standard or Natural for the mouse wheel and the trackpad separately. Out of the box your wheel scrolls like a PC and your trackpad stays natural.

⇧

Shift + wheel stays vertical

macOS turns Shift + mouse wheel into sideways scrolling, so your hotbar runs backwards while you sneak. Not any more. Tilt wheels and trackpad swipes still scroll sideways.

⌃

Control-click stays a left click

Bind Control to sprint or crouch and macOS turns your attack into a right click. Switch this on and a Control-click stays a left click.

Optional · off by default

Engineered for zero latency

Every app that changes input on a Mac adds a small round trip to the events it touches. Mouse Gaming Mode is built so your inputs skip that round trip whenever they can.

⚡︎

Out of the input path

Mouse movement and clicks never pass through it. The scroll hook stays switched off and turns on only at the moment a fix is needed: while you hold Shift, or while your fingers are on the trackpad.

◎

Watches without waiting

It knows when you hold Shift or touch the trackpad by listening alongside your inputs, which macOS never waits for. Even when a fix is working, the round trip is about a tenth of a millisecond.

▢

Featherweight

About 300 KB, 0% CPU while idle, no background polling, no windows, no network code and no analytics. It never touches your frame rate.

Set up in a minute

  1. Install Mouse Gaming Mode from the Mac App Store and open it. A mouse icon appears in your menu bar.
  2. Allow it in Accessibility when asked. That's what lets it change scroll and click events.
  3. Allow Input Monitoring for zero-latency mode. It lets the app notice Shift, Control and trackpad touches without slowing them down.
  4. For a zero-latency mouse wheel, turn Natural Scrolling off in System Settings › Trackpad. macOS then scrolls your mouse like a PC on its own, and Mouse Gaming Mode keeps your trackpad natural, stepping in only while you touch it. The menu walks you through it.

Questions

Will sneaking or sprinting still work in my game?

Yes. Games read the Shift and Control keys directly. Mouse Gaming Mode only removes them from the scroll or click event itself, which is what macOS uses to turn a scroll sideways or a click into a right click.

Does it affect my frame rate or aim?

No. It never sees mouse movement, uses no CPU while idle and draws nothing but its menu bar icon.

Shift + scroll no longer scrolls sideways in other apps.

That's the fix doing its job everywhere. If you rely on Shift + scroll for sideways scrolling, switch off Keep Shift + Wheel Vertical when you're not gaming.

I use another scroll-reversing app.

Quit it first, otherwise both reverse your scrolling and it ends up back where it started.

Does it work with a Magic Mouse?

A Magic Mouse scrolls smoothly, like a trackpad, so the App Store edition can't tell the two apart: a Magic Mouse follows your Trackpad setting.
