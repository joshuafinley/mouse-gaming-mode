Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

The short version

Mouse Gaming Mode collects no data. Nothing you do with it leaves your Mac.

What the app does with your input

To work, Mouse Gaming Mode sees scroll events. It also notices when Shift is pressed and when your fingers are on the trackpad. It does not read which other keys you press or record your typing. It changes these events on your Mac as they pass and keeps nothing: no logs, no history, no recordings.

No network, no analytics, no accounts

The app contains no networking code. It sends nothing to us or to anyone else, has no analytics or crash reporting of its own, shows no ads and needs no account.

What is stored

Only your menu choices (such as which device scrolls naturally), saved in the app's own preferences on your Mac. Deleting the app deletes them.

Permissions

macOS asks you to allow Mouse Gaming Mode in Accessibility, which lets it change scroll events, and in Input Monitoring, which lets it notice Shift and trackpad touches so it can stay out of the way the rest of the time. You can remove either permission at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Apple

If you download the app from the Mac App Store, Apple handles the purchase and may share anonymous crash and usage statistics with developers if you have chosen to share them with app developers in System Settings. That is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions are welcome on the support page.
