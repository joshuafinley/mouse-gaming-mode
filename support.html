Support

Something not working, or an idea for the app? Open an issue on GitHub and say which Mac, macOS version and mouse you use. Answers to the common questions are below.

Nothing happens when I scroll

  • Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and make sure Mouse Gaming Mode is switched on. The menu shows Allow in Accessibility… while it's missing.
  • Check Enabled is ticked in the menu.
  • Quit any other scroll-reversing app: two of them undo each other.

What is zero-latency mode?

With Input Monitoring allowed, the app's scroll hook stays switched off until a fix is needed: while you hold Shift, or while your fingers are on the trackpad. The rest of the time your inputs reach your games directly.

If your mouse wheel needs reversing because macOS's Natural Scrolling is on, every wheel scroll has to pass through the app, which adds about a tenth of a millisecond. Choose Make the Mouse Zero-Latency… in the menu: turning Natural Scrolling off in System Settings › Mouse lets macOS scroll your mouse like a PC on its own, and the app keeps your trackpad natural instead.

Shift + scroll doesn't scroll sideways any more

That's Keep Shift + Wheel Vertical working in every app. Switch it off in the menu when you want sideways scrolling back. Tilt wheels and thumb wheels keep scrolling sideways either way.

My Magic Mouse follows the trackpad setting

A Magic Mouse scrolls smoothly like a trackpad, and the App Store edition can't tell the two apart. Set the Trackpad direction to the one you want for both.

Starting with your Mac

Tick Open at Login in the menu. It also appears in System Settings › General › Login Items.
